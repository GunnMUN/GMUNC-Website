Policy
Payments will be completed through Gunn High School's Webstore. Please note that GMUNC's payment tab will be added closer to the conference date. For first time users, click the "How to Login to the Webstore" and follow the instructions. The payment is due the day of the conference (October 24), in which you will be given a paper copy of your delegation's invoice. If the payment is not completed on time, your delegation will not be allowed to participate in GMUNC XIII. Furthermore, each delegate must complete a delegate form and emergency contact and medical form. Such forms can be found in the "Conference" tab of this website. Any delegations requiring invoices in order to process a payment should contact us at secretariat@gunnmun.org.
